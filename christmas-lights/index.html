Christmas Light Installation FAQ

Everything you need to know about our turnkey holiday lighting service.

Do I provide the lights or do you?

We provide all commercial-grade, custom-cut lighting, timers, hardware, and commercial outdoor extension cords. We do not install customer-supplied store lights because store-bought lights cannot be custom tailored to your rooflines and do not meet our strict weatherproofing and safety standards.

What happens if a bulb burns out or storms displace a clip?

All packages include our 100% Season-Long Maintenance Guarantee. If a bulb burns out or wind shifts a light, simply text or call our team and we will come out within 24 hours to repair it at zero additional cost.

When do you take the lights down?

Takedown begins in early January through mid-February. You do not need to be home for takedown. We carefully label each custom-cut strand specifically for your home and store them safely in our company storage unit until next holiday season.

Why book before December 1st?

Our 50% OFF Early Bird Special + FREE Takedown & Storage offer strictly ends on December 1st. Because we only take on 150 homes per season across Tampa Bay to maintain five-star quality, spots fill up rapidly!
