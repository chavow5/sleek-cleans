Paver Sealing

Florida rain and UV rays quickly erode unsealed brick pavers, causing color fading, joint sand displacement, and weed growth. Sleek's 3-stage paver restoration process cleans deeply, replenishes silica joint sand, and applies commercial-grade protective sealant that resists oil, grease, and UV damage.
